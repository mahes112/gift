🔐 A story our hearts wrote together

5

Our Story Begins ❤️

It all began on a peaceful day near a temple. I was riding my bicycle with my cousins, laughing and enjoying the moment, when I saw him for the first time. Yash was riding his bicycle with his friends. In that single glance, something changed inside me. I didn’t know how to explain it then, but my heart knew—it was love at first sight. I wanted to speak to him, but I hesitated. From that day on, I started coming to the temple every day, not just out of devotion, but with the quiet hope of seeing him again

When Fate Stepped In

One unexpected day, Yash came and spoke to me and my cousins. I was surprised, nervous, and happy all at once. I smiled and spoke back, and in that moment, a stranger slowly became a friend. We started talking more and sharing about our lives. Soon, I discovered something unbelievable—we were studying in the same school, living on the same street, and our houses were right opposite each other. It felt like fate had been quietly connecting us long before we realized it

Love Finds Its Way

When school reopened, we began going to school together every day. Those simple walks became my favorite part of the day. One day, Yash gathered the courage to express his love and proposed to me. I already knew my feelings, but I wasn’t ready then, so I said no. Time passed, emotions grew stronger, and on September 30, 2017, I finally listened to my heart and said yes. That was the day our love story truly began. We talked every day, shared dreams, and slowly fell deeper in love. Later, we planned to meet at a coffee shop. I went with my sister, feeling excited and nervous, and that day became one of my happiest memories. We laughed, talked, and captured our joy in countless selfies.

A Love That Chose Forever

On October 11, 2022, my 18th birthday, Yash surprised me in the most beautiful way—he brought an eight-foot teddy bear, exactly my height. I was overwhelmed, and in that moment, all I could do was hug him tightly. Years passed, bringing challenges and patience, but our love never faded. Finally, on November 22, 2025, I gathered the courage to tell my parents about my love. Those two days were emotionally difficult, but love won. My parents accepted my story, trusted my choice, and decided to give my hand to the one who had always held my heart—Yash. What began with a single glance near a temple became a lifelong promise, and today, I carry this love into my forever

Every moment we’ve shared has led us here. This story is ready to move forward, waiting for one simple answer from you. 💍❤️
